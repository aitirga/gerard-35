# The castle of La Reina Bubbaloo

The last block of the game, designed with Aitor on 2026-11-19 and built since in a first version
(§ Built so far says what stands and what is a placeholder). Its three bosses, the possession of
Aitor, the artilugio and the endings are in [castle-bosses.md](castle-bosses.md).

It builds on what exists: the four zones of the palace are built as places
([places/palace.md](places/palace.md), [places/palace-zones.md](places/palace-zones.md)), with no
bosses and no flags. It **replaces** three things the docs held until now:

- The dungeon was fixed ("the roguelike idea was dropped in round 16"). It is **procedural again**,
  between fixed rooms.
- Each zone had its own teacher boss. There are **two bosses and the Queen**.
- The throne was open from day one. It is reached **only after the second boss**.

## Structure

```
Rec Comtal → the hall (the guards, the curtain)
  → the curtain's stair ─ phase 1, generated ─ GIMNASIO · Aurelià        ⟶ stage 2 of growth
  → (entry: Aurelià's room) ─ phase 2 ─ DIRECCIÓ · Mar, Montse, Pilar     ⟶ the definitivas
  → (entry: Direcció) ─ phase 3 ─ TRONO · La Reina Bubbaloo
```

- **The way in, the first time** (decided with Aitor 2026-11-21, `cortina` in `src/story.ts`). The
  hall's guards stand before the gum curtain; without ánimas they cannot be beaten, and the fall is
  the chapter's first lesson. Beaten once, they are **gone for good**: the hall is drawn again with
  the curtain tied back, and its stair, which in the dream climbed to the throne, climbs to the
  school's first floor, phase 1's generated floor (Gerard: *"Esto es el primer piso de la escuela. O
  lo era"*). Its way back comes down the same stair. The hall's east door still opens onto the patio,
  to look round the school, but the gym's door is held shut from inside (`gymShut`) until Aurelià
  falls: he is reached through the floor. After him the stair comes out by his gym, a school again.
- **The built zones are the fixed rooms.** The hall's curtain is where phase 1 starts, the gym is Aurelià's
  room, *Direcció* (off the secretaría, whose door the management team waits before) is the team's, the throne room is hers. What is
  generated lies between them.
- **Checkpoints.** A boss beaten stays beaten, and from then on every entry to the castle starts in
  that boss's room: won, greyed, drifting back to being a school (the proposal already in
  places/palace.md § identity). The palace is crossed "by the checkpoints its bosses leave"
  (chapter-2.md), and these are the two.
- **Falling** in the castle still ends the day: Gerard wakes at home (decided, chapter-2.md).
- **A boss beaten: her scream, white, and bed** (decided with Aitor 2026-11-20; `screamed` in
  `src/story.ts`, `LINES_CASTLE.screams`, `#queen-scream` in `src/story-ui.ts`). No night follows a
  castle boss and nobody walks home: with the boss's last words said, the Queen screams through the
  whole building, her words across the screen in her pink, shaking, more afraid with each boss
  (Aurelià: *"No. NO! Torneu-me'l! Era meu!"*; Dirección, bigger: *"Un altre cop no! No us
  n'aneu! NO EM DEIXEU AQUÍ SOLA!"*), the picture goes to white, and out of the white they are in
  bed in the morning: the 22nd again, as every morning of the loop (the day's count goes on, as it
  does after any night). The night is for coming back from the friends' missions and for the
  afternoons spent (chapter-2.md § Night and the way home), not for her castle.
- **The castle changes with each scream** (§ The castle after each boss): what lies behind a beaten
  boss goes back to being a school, what lies ahead is more hers than before, and the door that
  leads on is clotted with her gum.
- **Flags** (they replace the proposed `zona-patio`, `zona-gimnasio`, `zona-secretaria`):
  `cortina` (the guards beaten), `aurelia`, `direccion`, and the ending's (`aitor-poseido`, `artilugio`, `reina`). Each gets its
  step in `STORY_ARCS`, its dot on the picker's timeline and its place in `dev-chapter`.
- **Gates stay diegetic**: the door past the gym says why it will not open until Aurelià has
  fallen, and Direcció's the same. No panel says where to go.

## The generated floor

Each phase is **one continuous floor**, walked like any place: no loading between rooms.

- **Rooms and corridors.** Rooms are square or rectangular, 5×5 to 9×9 cells, laid on a coarse
  lattice and joined by corridors two cells wide (the party walks two abreast and a chaser must be
  able to pass). A **main path** of N rooms from the entry to the boss's antechamber, and side
  branches off it.
- **The seed is the save, the day and the phase.** The floor is the same all day and changes the
  next: since a fall ends the day, every attempt after one meets a new floor. It is generated by a
  seeded generator (the game already has one, `seeded` in `src/battle-sim.ts`) and checked for a
  way through with `reachable` (`src/street-shift.ts`).
- **Kinds of room**: fight, corridor, élite (La Delegada, § Bestiary), **rest** (one per floor: a
  staff room, which cures at the cost of time), **find** (an object) and the boss's antechamber.
- **Each pupil is a body of its own and hunts its own way**, the same rules as out on the Meridiana
  at night (`src/night-pupils.ts`), with the floor's own numbers (`src/castle-pupils.ts`): what the
  way home rehearsed is what the rooms ask. They can be seen coming, dodged or met; a fight is a
  choice more often than not.
  - A room's pupils walk its round until they have Gerard; lost, they go and look where he was, then
    back to their room. Walking into one, or one reaching the party, is its room's fight.
  - El Repetidor winds up and charges across a room (a wall is always near to crash him into), La
    Chivata keeps her distance and points him out to whoever is a room or two away, Los Gemelos cut
    the corner ahead, El Castigado faces the wall until a run is heard nearby, El Montón holds on
    for a room and a half.
  - **La Delegada waits in her room.** Once she has seen Gerard, or La Chivata has pointed him out
    within her reach, she walks at him and never stops, and nobody gives up while she stands.
  - **El Ausente is only seen inside the light the party carries**, or once it comes at them.
  - **The staff room is the floor's safe place**, as home's portal is the street's: pupils do not go
    in and nobody in it is seen.
  - The first floor is in daylight; on the second and third the pupils see little outside the
    party's light and run faster (`FLOOR_TIERS`), and they glow a little, as they do at night.
  - Nothing to hide behind yet: what cuts a line of sight is a wall, a corner, a corridor. El
    Empollón, who learns the hiding places on the street, has nothing to learn inside (to settle).
- **No map tells the way.** The map shows the rooms walked and where the party is, never the route.

How it could meet the code (for when it is built): one place per phase whose grid is generated on
entry and put in place of `ENVIRONMENTS[...]`'s, as `src/street-shift.ts` already swaps a grid
live; a generic dressing in `buildPalaceZone` driven by a theme that is a mix of two `ZoneTheme`s.

## The art drifts towards the boss

Each room is dressed by **how far along the main path it is**: the first is the zone the party
comes from, the last already belongs to the boss. Walking the floor is watching the place become
someone's.

| Phase | Rooms on the path | From → to | Light | What shows up along the way |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 8 | Court green (the patio) → mat blue, marble and laurel | Day | Lanes painted on the floor, busts on plinths, a timeline running along the wall, a stopwatch heard before it is seen |
| 2 | 10 | Mat blue → manila and steel (the secretaría, where Direcció's door is), in the dark | Emergency lighting | Filing cabinets, stamps, tubes that fail, leaks, minutes pinned to the walls |
| 3 | 12 | Manila → night indigo and gold (the throne), in decay | Almost none | Old cracked gum, pink gone dull, playground things worn out |

- Each phase drifts between **two identity cards that already exist** (visual-style.md), so no new
  dominant hue is needed and neighbours still contrast: a floor never looks like one zone, it
  looks like the way from one to the next.
- **Her pink** stays under a tenth of the screen in phases 1 and 2. In phase 3 it rises, but old
  and desaturated: saturated pink is still only her. **Violet** is still only possession: Aurelià's
  eyes, the team's, Aitor's.
- A phase's rooms count and the numbers above are a first guess, to tune by playing.

## Bestiary: the Queen's pupils

One family for the whole castle, few and well defined: **eight, not thirty**. They are not school
objects and they do not change theme by phase. They are **gum in the shape of a pupil**: figures a
child's height, of stretched bubbaloo, a smock hinted by the folds and, where the face would be, a
bubble. They are not real children and not possessed people (violet stays reserved): they are
hers, pink. They unsettle; they are not funny. Each is a kind of pupil any teacher knows, and that
character **is** how it fights.

| Pupil | Character → how it fights | Question → answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **El Repetidor** | Big and slow. Announces *Empujón → X* and hits very hard. Wrapped in thick pringue (first year) or a crust (third) | **Moment** and **element**: Defender on the tell; any blow of fire melts the pringue; the crust gives after two physical blows, or while it is wet |
| **El Empollón** | Studies the party: after taking a kind of blow it *learns* it and resists it (a chip on its plate). The kind it has not learned it does not know: «No se lo sabe», ×1.5. Confused, it forgets | **Kind of blow**: alternate physical and magic, never repeat; or **status**: confuse it |
| **La Chivata** | Fragile. *Chivatazo → X* marks one of the party: the next blow they take is tripled. Blinded she cannot point («No ve a quién») | **Who first**: her, before El Repetidor hits; or **status**: blind her |
| **Los Gemelos** | Two bodies. If one falls alone, the other holds it up a whole round more and gets it up at the end of the next one (2026-11-23: at the end of the same round it was too tight). Weak to electricity, and a spark on one passes to the other («Pasa al otro») | **Order and moment**: fell the other before the next round is over; a spark does it for both |
| **El Ausente** | Not there: it cannot be hit except in the round it has just acted. Clock statuses do reach it. Wet, it shows («Se le ve») | **Moment**, or **status**: burn it and let the clock work, or wet it and hit it when it likes |
| **El Castigado** | Facing the wall, it does not attack. Every blow that does not fell it gives it *Rabia*; at three it bursts on everyone | **Moment**: leave it for last and fell it in one big blow (an open wound, a dual, Punto débil) |
| **El Montón** | One of the bunch (*uno del montón*): gum with no shape of its own. It takes the shape of the first blow that lands on it, what it carried and how hard it was, and gives it back every turn. Shaped, it is weak to what undoes its shape (the ring below; a wound's shape, to fire) | **Choose what it is shown**: open with a light blow that carries nothing, or give it a shape the party can undo |
| **Élite · La Delegada** | In charge. While she stands, hers all strike the same one and their tells do not show | **Part and order**: silence her (confusión, a lost turn) or fell her first |

Every rule above is by **kind of blow or status, never by ánima** (combat.md), shows on the plate
(a chip, a tell, a count), and has an answer within reach of the party the phase is met with.

### Three years, one rule a phase

The bubbaloo itself: gum outside, a filling inside. The same eight pupils come back each phase a
year older, and the gum changes what the player knew.

| Year | Phase | The gum | What changes for the player |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First | 1 | Fresh | Fire melts it: the first lesson of the game still holds |
| Second | 2 | With a **filling**: each pupil carries an element inside, seen through its gum (orange, blue or yellow) and named on its plate | Weak to what undoes its filling, resists its own. It **hits with its filling** (burns, wets, or shocks whoever is wet) and, when it falls, **spills it on the other pupils**: fire sets them alight, water wets them, a spark runs through the wet ones |
| Third | 3 | **Rancid**, grey and cracked | Fire no longer melts it: it **breaks under physical blows**. **Wet, it softens**: while it is wet it stops resisting fire and is weak to it. What was learned first is turned over |

### Weaknesses (2026-11-21)

Until this round nearly everything in the castle was weak to fire, the second year's weakness could
not be seen and the third year had none. Now there are three kinds of weakness, all with the same
pop («¡Débil!» / «Resiste») and the same chip on the plate (docs/combat.md § Debilidades):

| Kind | What it is | In the castle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Element** | Fire, water, electricity (×1.5 / ×0.5) | Fresh gum to fire; a filling to what undoes it; Los Gemelos to electricity; El Atril to fire and electricity |
| **Kind of blow** | Physical, as a weakness of its own | Rancid gum; El Empollón to the kind it has not learned |
| **Combination** | A status on the foe changes what it is | Wet: rancid gum softens, El Ausente shows, the crust gives. Blinded: La Chivata and La Delegada. Confused: El Empollón forgets. Shaped: El Montón |

One rule runs through the whole castle, **the ring** (`UNDOES` in `src/castle-fight.ts`): water puts
out fire, a spark runs through water, fire melts the wire. The second year's fillings, El Montón's
shapes, Dirección's folders and the Queen's filled layers all follow it: it is learned on the second
floor and asked twice more.

- A foe's weaknesses are worked out from what it is **now** (`castleAffinity`), not fixed when the
  fight is made: wetting a third-year changes its plate.
- An element is always **a prize, never a requirement**: every room also has its answer of order,
  kind of blow or status, within reach of the least a party can bring (fire, physical, astral,
  Sangrado). Astral (magic with no element) never meets a weakness nor a resistance: with more
  resistances about, that is its worth.
- The hall's guards are fresh gum: fire, as before.

- **Phase 1** has four (El Repetidor, El Empollón, La Chivata, Los Gemelos) and La Delegada near
  its end; **phase 2** adds El Ausente and El Castigado; **phase 3**, El Montón. Each phase brings
  something new without changing family.
- **Difficulty comes from how they combine, not from how many there are.** La Chivata with El
  Repetidor (the mark and the shove), El Empollón with El Castigado (no blow twice, and it must
  fall in one), La Delegada with Los Gemelos, El Ausente with El Repetidor (one blow told a turn
  ahead, one never told but on a rhythm), El Montón with La Chivata (whatever it was shown comes
  back, and her mark triples it). Each room is a combination with its own question.
- From phase 2 on a room is **not meant to be won the first time**: it kills a party that has not
  read it, and the fall costs the day. The second meeting is the one that is played.
- Each combination keeps its pair of `TARGETS` (`src/battle-sim.ts`), and each pupil and La
  Delegada can be met from the fight tester.

### Encarnados (Aitor, 2026-11-21)

Some of her pupils come harder than the rest: **a warm red cast over their gum and a faint red halo
at their feet** (toned down on 2026-11-21: they read at a glance without turning the room red).
They are the same pupils and the same room, with the same question, and less room for mistakes.

- **One group every other day** (even days; Aitor, 2026-11-21: fewer of them). By day it is one of the plain rooms of the castle's floor, another each day
  (`strongRoom` in `src/castle-story.ts`); at night, on the Meridiana, one of the groups out
  (`STRONG.group` in `src/castle.ts`). Whichever is beaten first is the day's: after it nobody is red
  until the next morning.
- **What they are**: every pupil of the group has `STRONG.pv` times its PV (1.3) and hits
  `STRONG.power` times as hard (1.1), on top of its year and, at night, of the night's strength
  (`castleStrong` in `src/castle-fight.ts`). Bosses and their parts are never Encarnados. They were
  ×1.6 and ×1.3 until the final pass: the rooms themselves are harder now ([fight-sim.md](fight-sim.md) §
  The castle, as tuned, which has every room's figures), and on top of that the old numbers made a
  room nobody won.
- **What they leave**: beaten, an **Eco de luz**, which flies into the party as a friend's does
  (`strongWon`; flags `luz-encarnado-1` to `-6`, `STRONG_LIGHTS`). One every other day and **six in a
  game**: when the sixth is taken they do not come out again. Lost against, nothing is taken and they are
  still there.
- **How they are told apart**: by looking. The gum takes a warm red cast and the halo is on
  the floor, walking the floor or the avenue and in the fight (`encarnar`, `redden` and
  `encarnadoHalo` in `src/castle-models.ts`); the plate carries a mark that lasts the fight
  («Encarnado: más entero y pega más. Vencido, suelta un Eco de luz.»). The red is an ember's,
  leaning to orange: the saturated pink stays the Queen's and violet stays possession's
  (visual-style.md). Nobody announces them and nothing points at them.
- **What they are for**: something to go and get when a boss says no. Measured (`just game-sim`,
  nine games): zero to two a game for players who do not look for them; whoever goes out for them
  can have the six.
- **Where to try them**: the fight tester's *Alumnos › Encarnados* (rooms and the night), the
  picker's arc *Encarnados*, `just fight-sim run --fight alumnos-2d:encarnados` (any room, or
  `night-guard:encarnados`), `just screenshots encarnados`, and each pupil's row in the bestiary.
  Their pair of `TARGETS`: `encarnados-bien` and `encarnados-sin-pensar`.

### Ecos of the rooms (Aitor, 2026-11-21)

Each of the castle's rooms, each combination of pupils and each year's La Delegada, lets go of an
**Eco de luz the first time it is beaten** (`ROOM_LIGHTS` in `src/castle.ts`, flags `luz-alumnos-1a`…
`luz-delegada-3`, granted in `castleBattle`): fifteen in all. The floor is generated each day, so
they are found by coming back and going through rooms not seen yet: the little farming each block
asks for, paid. Not at night (only inside), and a room already beaten gives nothing more. The
picker's arc *Las aulas* marks them a floor at a time.

### How they look (proposal, 2026-11-19)

Drawn with `just character-3d` (`tools/figures.toml`, from `[chivata]` on; the sheet is
`output/characters/3d/alumnos-sheet.png`). Aitor asked for more fear, a clear climb from small to
big, and pupils that belong to the bosses they lead to. Not a toy, unlike the cast: a sculpted
horror figure of matte gum in the game's world.

- **The family**: chewed gum in a pupil's shape, the smock only folds and a hem, feet melting into
  strings on the floor. **No face**: a bubble where it should be, with a face pressed into it from
  inside. The bubble is the only saturated pink on them (hers).
- **They grow with the years.** A first-year pupil is a child's size; the second year is taller and
  lankier; the third, rotten and as big as a worn-out grown man. Growing up and losing yourself in it
  is the game's subject, and the castle shows it on its own pupils.

## The castle after each boss

Built 2026-11-20 (`src/castle-mood.ts`, `dressMood` in `src/palace-zones.ts`, `mood` in a
`ZoneTheme`). The castle is four stretches, each ending in a boss: the patio and the gym
(Aurelià), the first floor's rooms with them; the secretaría (Mar, Montse and Pilar); the throne.
Each scream moves every stretch one step, and the party sees it the next time it goes in.

| Stretch | Its boss stands | Its boss has fallen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Colour | The zone's own palette, sunk towards a dark plum and pulled to her pink, one step more for each scream so far | The same rooms with most of the colour taken out: a school again, greyed |
| Her gum | Runs down the tall walls and pools at their feet from the first scream; black rot in it from the second | None: every gum part of the zone is gone |
| Light | Dimmer with each scream | Plain daylight, no tint of hers |
| The door onward | — | While the next boss stands, the door that leads to it (the gym's changing rooms, Direcció's door) is clotted with her gum, veined over the wall around it, lit by a pulsing pink lamp; black blisters from the second scream |

- The generated floors carry the same count: the first is met with no scream, the second with one,
  the third with two (`castle-floor.ts`).
- Nothing says it. No line, no panel: the party walks back in through a gym that is a gym again and
  finds the next door grown over.
- A stretch is only ever seen by the party at one count before its boss falls (the checkpoint moves
  the way in past it), so what reads in play is: each stretch further in is worse than the last,
  what is behind is given back, and the way on is marked by her and by nothing else.
