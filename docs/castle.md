# The castle of La Reina Bubbaloo

The last block of the game, designed with Aitor on 2026-11-19. **Design only: nothing here is
built.** Each piece is built later, one at a time, with his OK. Its three bosses, the possession of
Aitor, the artilugio and the endings are in [castle-bosses.md](castle-bosses.md).

It builds on what exists: the four zones of the palace are built as places
([places/palace.md](places/palace.md), [places/palace-zones.md](places/palace-zones.md)), with no
bosses and no flags. It **replaces** three things the docs held until now:

- The dungeon was fixed ("the roguelike idea was dropped in round 16"). It is **procedural again**,
  between fixed rooms.
- Each zone had its own teacher boss. There are **two bosses and the Queen**.
- The throne was open from day one. It is reached **only after the second boss**.

## Structure

```
Rec Comtal → the hall (the guards, the curtain)
  → PATIO ─ phase 1, generated ─ GIMNASIO · Aurelià                      ⟶ stage 2 of growth
  → (entry: Aurelià's room) ─ phase 2 ─ DIRECCIÓ · Mar, Montse, Pilar     ⟶ the definitivas
  → (entry: Direcció) ─ phase 3 ─ TRONO · La Reina Bubbaloo
```

- **The built zones are the fixed rooms.** The patio is where phase 1 starts, the gym is Aurelià's
  room, the secretaría's *Direcció* is the management team's, the throne room is hers. What is
  generated lies between them.
- **Checkpoints.** A boss beaten stays beaten, and from then on every entry to the castle starts in
  that boss's room: won, greyed, drifting back to being a school (the proposal already in
  places/palace.md § identity). The palace is crossed "by the checkpoints its bosses leave"
  (chapter-2.md), and these are the two.
- **Falling** in the castle still ends the day: Gerard wakes at home (decided, chapter-2.md).
- **A boss beaten: her scream, white, and bed** (decided with Aitor 2026-11-20; `screamed` in
  `src/story.ts`, `LINES_CASTLE.screams`, `#queen-scream` in `src/story-ui.ts`). No night follows a
  castle boss and nobody walks home: with the boss's last words said, the Queen screams through the
  whole building, her words across the screen in her pink, shaking, more afraid with each boss
  (Aurelià: *"No. ¡NO! ¡Devolvédmelo! ¡Era mío!"*; Dirección, bigger: *"¡Otra vez no! ¡No os
  vayáis! ¡NO ME DEJÉIS AQUÍ SOLA!"*), the picture goes to white, and out of the white they are in
  bed in the morning: the 22nd again, as every morning of the loop (the day's count goes on, as it
  does after any night). The night is for coming back from the friends' missions and for the
  afternoons spent (chapter-2.md § Night and the way home), not for her castle.
- **The castle hardens with each scream** (planned, not built): after each boss the building itself
  looks more threatening, its textures further along from school towards hers, so the fear in the
  scream is seen on the walls the next time they go in. Today only each phase's own drift exists
  (§ The art drifts towards the boss).
- **Flags** (they replace the proposed `zona-patio`, `zona-gimnasio`, `zona-secretaria`):
  `aurelia`, `direccion`, and the ending's (`aitor-poseido`, `artilugio`, `reina`). Each gets its
  step in `STORY_ARCS`, its dot on the picker's timeline and its place in `dev-chapter`.
- **Gates stay diegetic**: the door past the gym says why it will not open until Aurelià has
  fallen, and Direcció's the same. No panel says where to go.

## The generated floor

Each phase is **one continuous floor**, walked like any place: no loading between rooms.

- **Rooms and corridors.** Rooms are square or rectangular, 5×5 to 9×9 cells, laid on a coarse
  lattice and joined by corridors two cells wide (the party walks two abreast and a chaser must be
  able to pass). A **main path** of N rooms from the entry to the boss's antechamber, and side
  branches off it.
- **The seed is the save, the day and the phase.** The floor is the same all day and changes the
  next: since a fall ends the day, every attempt after one meets a new floor. It is generated by a
  seeded generator (the game already has one, `seeded` in `src/battle-sim.ts`) and checked for a
  way through with `reachable` (`src/street-shift.ts`).
- **Kinds of room**: fight, corridor, élite (La Delegada, § Bestiary), **rest** (one per floor: a
  staff room, which cures at the cost of time), **find** (an object) and the boss's antechamber.
- **The pupils roam** as chasers (`src/chasers.ts`, as the tower's fears do; out on the Meridiana at
  night each one hunts its own way instead, `src/night-pupils.ts`):
  they can be seen coming, dodged or met. A fight is a choice more often than not.
- **No map tells the way.** The map shows the rooms walked and where the party is, never the route.

How it could meet the code (for when it is built): one place per phase whose grid is generated on
entry and put in place of `ENVIRONMENTS[...]`'s, as `src/street-shift.ts` already swaps a grid
live; a generic dressing in `buildPalaceZone` driven by a theme that is a mix of two `ZoneTheme`s.

## The art drifts towards the boss

Each room is dressed by **how far along the main path it is**: the first is the zone the party
comes from, the last already belongs to the boss. Walking the floor is watching the place become
someone's.

| Phase | Rooms on the path | From → to | Light | What shows up along the way |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 8 | Court green (the patio) → mat blue, marble and laurel | Day | Lanes painted on the floor, busts on plinths, a timeline running along the wall, a stopwatch heard before it is seen |
| 2 | 10 | Mat blue → manila and steel (the secretaría), in the dark | Emergency lighting | Filing cabinets, stamps, tubes that fail, leaks, minutes pinned to the walls |
| 3 | 12 | Manila → night indigo and gold (the throne), in decay | Almost none | Old cracked gum, pink gone dull, playground things worn out |

- Each phase drifts between **two identity cards that already exist** (visual-style.md), so no new
  dominant hue is needed and neighbours still contrast: a floor never looks like one zone, it
  looks like the way from one to the next.
- **Her pink** stays under a tenth of the screen in phases 1 and 2. In phase 3 it rises, but old
  and desaturated: saturated pink is still only her. **Violet** is still only possession: Aurelià's
  eyes, the team's, Aitor's.
- A phase's rooms count and the numbers above are a first guess, to tune by playing.

## Bestiary: the Queen's pupils

One family for the whole castle, few and well defined: **eight, not thirty**. They are not school
objects and they do not change theme by phase. They are **gum in the shape of a pupil**: figures a
child's height, of stretched bubbaloo, a smock hinted by the folds and, where the face would be, a
bubble. They are not real children and not possessed people (violet stays reserved): they are
hers, pink. They unsettle; they are not funny. Each is a kind of pupil any teacher knows, and that
character **is** how it fights.

| Pupil | Character → how it fights | Question → answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **El Repetidor** | Big and slow. Announces *Empujón → X* and hits very hard. Wrapped in thick pringue | **Moment** and **element**: Defender on the tell; melt the pringue |
| **El Empollón** | Studies the party: after taking a kind of blow it *learns* it and resists it (a chip on its plate) | **Kind of blow**: alternate physical and magic; never repeat |
| **La Chivata** | Fragile. *Chivatazo → X* marks one of the party: the next blow they take is tripled | **Who first**: her, before El Repetidor hits |
| **Los Gemelos** | Two bodies. If one falls alone, the other gets it up at the end of the round | **Order and moment**: bring them down together and fell both in one round |
| **El Ausente** | Not there: it cannot be hit except in the round it has just acted. Clock statuses do reach it | **Moment**, or **status**: burn it and let the clock work |
| **El Castigado** | Facing the wall, it does not attack. Every blow that does not fell it gives it *Rabia*; at three it bursts on everyone | **Moment**: leave it for last and fell it in one big blow (an open wound, a dual, Punto débil) |
| **El Montón** | One of the bunch (*uno del montón*): gum with no shape of its own. It takes the shape of the first blow that lands on it, what it carried and how hard it was, and gives it back every turn | **Choose what it is shown**: open with a light blow that carries nothing |
| **Élite · La Delegada** | In charge. While she stands, hers all strike the same one and their tells do not show | **Part and order**: silence her (confusión, a lost turn) or fell her first |

Every rule above is by **kind of blow or status, never by ánima** (combat.md), shows on the plate
(a chip, a tell, a count), and has an answer within reach of the party the phase is met with.

### Three years, one rule a phase

The bubbaloo itself: gum outside, a filling inside. The same eight pupils come back each phase a
year older, and the gum changes what the player knew.

| Year | Phase | The gum | What changes for the player |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First | 1 | Fresh | Fire melts it: the first lesson of the game still holds |
| Second | 2 | With a **filling**: each pupil carries an element inside, seen when it cracks | Weaknesses and reactions: the filling decides what to hit it with, and what it leaves when it falls |
| Third | 3 | **Rancid**, grey and cracked | Fire no longer melts it: it breaks under blows. What was learned first is turned over |

- **Phase 1** has four (El Repetidor, El Empollón, La Chivata, Los Gemelos) and La Delegada near
  its end; **phase 2** adds El Ausente and El Castigado; **phase 3**, El Montón. Each phase brings
  something new without changing family.
- **Difficulty comes from how they combine, not from how many there are.** La Chivata with El
  Repetidor (the mark and the shove), El Empollón with El Castigado (no blow twice, and it must
  fall in one), La Delegada with Los Gemelos, El Ausente with El Repetidor (one blow told a turn
  ahead, one never told but on a rhythm), El Montón with La Chivata (whatever it was shown comes
  back, and her mark triples it). Each room is a combination with its own question.
- From phase 2 on a room is **not meant to be won the first time**: it kills a party that has not
  read it, and the fall costs the day. The second meeting is the one that is played.
- Each combination keeps its pair of `TARGETS` (`src/battle-sim.ts`), and each pupil and La
  Delegada can be met from the fight tester.

### How they look (proposal, 2026-11-19)

Drawn with `just character-3d` (`tools/figures.toml`, from `[chivata]` on; the sheet is
`output/characters/3d/alumnos-sheet.png`). Aitor asked for more fear, a clear climb from small to
big, and pupils that belong to the bosses they lead to. Not a toy, unlike the cast: a sculpted
horror figure of matte gum in the game's world.

- **The family**: chewed gum in a pupil's shape, the smock only folds and a hem, feet melting into
  strings on the floor. **No face**: a bubble where it should be, with a face pressed into it from
  inside. The bubble is the only saturated pink on them (hers).
- **They grow with the years.** A first-year pupil is a child's size; the second year is taller and
  lankier; the third, rotten and as big as a worn-out grown man. Growing up and losing yourself in it
  is the game's subject, and the castle shows it on its own pupils.

| Year | Whose floor | The gum | Their mark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.º | Aurelià's (the gym) | Fresh: the pale flesh pink of chewed gum | Hands white with chalk (the blackboard's and the gym's); La Delegada wears his laurel |
| 2.º | Mar, Montse and Pilar's (Direcció) | Bruised plum, harder, cracked; the filling glows in fire orange, water blue or electric yellow, the three elements of the management team | A manila file tag on the wrist and a registry stamp on the chest: filed |
| 3.º | The Queen's | Hers, gone bad (Aitor: more gum, more contrast): loud saturated pink, swollen and stretched, eaten by black rot, black pustules and black veins | Bubbles half burst and black at the edges; nothing grey. Her saturated pink is allowed: they are her minions (visual-style.md) |

| Pupil | Size (m, first seen) | What only it has |
| --- | --- | --- |
| La Chivata | 1.0 | One arm stretched to twice her height, rope-thin, ending in a pointing finger |
| Los Gemelos | 1.1 each | Too tall and still; the bubble flattened on the side that pressed against the twin; a torn band of gum from the hand |
| El Empollón | 1.15 | A bubble far too big, a dark brain-like mass inside, scabs of crust where it learned a blow |
| La Delegada | 1.6 | Rigid and upright, a sash, Aurelià's laurel, threads of gum hanging from every finger |
| El Repetidor | 1.9 → 2.1 → 2.4 | A hulk with a small bubble sunk between the shoulders, rolls of crust like armour, boulder fists |
| El Ausente | 1.7 | An empty shell split open in front, empty sleeves, the bubble hanging like a veil |
| El Castigado | 2.2 | Bowed into its own chest; a hunch-bubble bigger than its torso glowing with the filling (its *Rabia*) |
| El Montón (was El Nuevo) | 2.6 | A heap of rotten pink gum with half-made arms and sunken face-bubbles; no legs |

In the game each figure keeps Meshy's own skeleton and clips (`src/castle-models.ts`, § Built so
far), and El Repetidor, drawn in all three years, wears the mesh of his year. To settle with Aitor:
whether the other pupils get a mesh per year, only a texture, or only their scale and tint. The
first year's look decides the rest.

Placeholders and decisions taken for now (each one Aitor's to change):

- Each pupil is drawn from its own figure once it has one (`just character-3d`: src/monsters/ or
  src/creatures/, loaded by `src/castle-models.ts` the first time a fight or a floor needs it), at
  its own height, El Repetidor by his year; in a fight it runs on Meshy's clip when it runs up and
  holds its pose standing, and on the floor it walks its round and runs the chase. Until its figure
  lands it is the palace minion's model at its own size, as Aurelià and Dirección are (the Queen has
  hers since 2026-11-20).
- One hour a fight inside the castle (`COSTS.castle`); a boss beaten ends the day there, with her scream (§ Structure).
- The Queen comes back at 40 % of her PV (`CASTLE.reinaVuelta`).
- Bernat's phone call stands in for the salón de juegos mission that finds the artilugio.
- The Acta announces its Resolución a turn ahead (docs/castle-bosses.md § Mar, Montse and Pilar),
  so holding on or breaking the quorum is the answer.
- The last three pupils (built 2026-11-19), each in a room of its own: *Ausente y Repetidor* and
  *Empollón y Castigado* on the second floor, *Montón y Chivata* on the third.
  - **El Ausente** keeps a rhythm: it strikes («Presente») in the rounds it is away, then stays the
    whole next round, the only one it can be hit in (its turn skipped), and goes. A blow while it is
    away does nothing, but what the blow lights still takes: a fire or a wound keeps working.
  - **El Castigado** takes no turn while it faces the wall; ticks and falls give no Rabia. Full, it
    bursts on its turn, so the partner can still fell it first, and goes back to the wall at nought.
  - **El Montón** reads "takes the shape of the first blow" whole: what the blow carried (an element,
    else a wound, else nothing), how hard it was (its PV, with the year on top) and how far it
    reached. Fire burns whoever it hits, water reaches everyone and leaves them wet, electricity
    leaves them in shock (a turn lost), a wound bleeds; a blow on everyone comes back on everyone.
    Its plate says what it will give back. Shapeless, it only leans on one, lightly.
- Not built: the definitivas, the outer rings and level caps (`levelCap` exists, unused), the snow
  globe inside, zones turning back into school once won, the six-note motif in the music, the
  Queen's final rules (left for last, docs/castle-bosses.md).

Measured with `just fight-sim check` (the castle's `TARGETS` in `src/battle-sim.ts`): with the dual
party at Papagato 3 and Atacar's three elements, playing the answer wins Aurelià 82 %, Dirección
92 %, the Queen with the artilugio 100 %; without it, 0 %. The first Queen is never won. The last
three pupils' rooms, playing the answer: 100 % each; without it, El Ausente's 8 %, El Castigado's
10 %, El Montón's 23 %: the bot that does not read it mostly opens with Las dos a la vez, a blow on
everyone that bleeds, and wins 17 % of those; the few times it shows it a plain blow, about 60 %.
