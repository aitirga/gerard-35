# Nuria · Cardedeu (working title)

Status: designing (round 10 next: assets, then open questions and the plan) · Last updated: 2026-10-24

## Summary

Nuria waits for Gerard and Aitor at the door of her building in Cardedeu, with Haru and Yuki at
her side. Uri, her partner, who never talks, has become a kid again (a bit wild, but a kid): he
won't stop talking and has run off down the street with Nil, their baby. Nuria wants to save
him and lends Gerard the cats: **Papagato** wakes up there, before the end, and Haru and Yuki
fight with the party through the mini-dungeon. Win, and Papagato stays with the party and the
cats come home with Gerard; give up, and they go back to Nuria. Round the corner the street turns into a mini-dungeon (a puzzle and a final boss). Nuria's
ánima is **Papagato**: it brings Haru and Yuki, the cats, to fight with the party.

## The maze (mini-dungeon)

Theme: **fear**, Uri's. Uri loves video games, so the maze plays like one: three levels, each
harder, and Uri (as a kid) narrates them from ahead, never the HUD: *"¡Nivel dos! ¡Ahora salen
más!"*. At each level Uri shows up ahead with Nil in his arms, violet-eyed, talking non-stop, and
runs off (*"¡No me pillas!"*): he is what pulls the player on.

| Level | Where | What gets harder | Monster |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · La calle | Jacint Verdaguer closed into a maze of parked cars, hedges and fences | Learn that monsters burst out of places | **El Cebollón** |
| 2 · Los patios | The yards of the terraced houses across the street | Fences move when Uri shouts *"¡cambio!"* | **El Plato Misterioso** |
| 3 · Hacia arriba | Stairs, balconies and ladders up Nuria's block | Vertical; looking down | **Game Over** |
| Top | The tiled roof of Nuria's block | — | **El Vértigo** (boss) |

**Ambushes are fair**: the place warns first (a bin lid rattling, a hedge shaking, a car boot
jumping). A player who looks can step around or brace. Proposal: walking into one starts the
fight; going round it does not.

**Monsters** (proposals; the user likes the three):

- **El Cebollón** (he hates onion): a huge onion in layers. Each hit peels one; while it has
  layers it makes everyone cry (they miss more). Multi-hit attacks (the cats' claws) peel it fast:
  the fight that teaches Papagato.
- **El Plato Misterioso** (he won't try new food): a dish under a cloche. Each round it lifts the
  lid and is a different food with a different weakness: look before you hit.
- **Game Over** (the video games of his childhood): a pixel ghost. If it is not beaten within a
  few rounds it "restarts" with full PV. Pressure to hit hard.

## Beats

1. Arrival: R4 to Cardedeu; Nuria waiting downstairs at her building, Haru and Yuki at her feet.
2. She tells them about Uri and Nil and **lends them the cats**: Papagato wakes up (ÁNIMA
   DESBLOQUEADA) and the mission is accepted. From here on, Haru and Yuki go with the party.
3. They follow Uri down the street. Round the corner the street turns into a **maze that gets
   harder as they go**, and **monsters burst out of places** along the way: things from Uri's
   childhood and his **fears**. The goal is to reach and catch Uri (puzzle + the mission's own
   dynamic). The theme of the whole mini-dungeon is **fear** (decided).
4. Boss: **El Vértigo** on the roof of Nuria's block; Uri beats his fear (see *Final boss*).
5. Win: Uri is himself again, Nil safe. Papagato stays with the party for good, Nuria's ánima
   light goes with Gerard, and **Haru and Yuki come home**: from then on they live in the
   Meridiana flat too.

- **Freedom always** (decided): they can leave, go home and come back as often as they like, but
  nothing is kept until it is won: leaving hands Papagato and the cats back to Nuria, who waits
  downstairs again. One of the first missions most players will do, but the order stays open.
- **Falling in the mini-dungeon** wakes Gerard at home ("¡NO!"), with the mission undone. It is
  meant to be hard for now; a passive later may soften it.

- Own mechanic, and how it is taught: **a maze of Uri's fears that grows harder**, with
  monsters that ambush from places that warn first (see *The maze*).

## Final boss

**El Vértigo** (Uri's fear of heights). Proposal, round 7.

- Where: the tiled hipped roof of Nuria's block. Uri has climbed up playing and is frozen on the
  ridge, Nil in his arms, violet eyes, silent for the first time all day.
- Look: a very tall, thin figure of stacked ladders and roof tiles, swaying, with a spiral eye that
  turns. It does not stand on the roof: it rises from the drop beside it.
- **Altura** (the fight's own rule): the roof goes up. It starts at 1 and the world shows it, not
  a bar: the street sinks away below, cars and trees shrink, the wind grows. The higher it is, the
  harder El Vértigo hits.
- Phase 1 · **Mirar abajo**. The spiral eye spins one round (the warning); next round it makes
  everyone look down: Altura +1 and **Mareo** (lowers Velocidad, stacks) on everyone who did not
  brace. Two answers: **Defender** that round (holding on to the tiles: no Mareo), or **hit the
  eye** while it spins to shut it. The cats never get dizzy (they walk the ridge): Papagato's
  skills are the party's answer to the eye.
- If Altura reaches 5: **Vacío**, a big hit to everyone, and Altura drops back to 3. Hard on
  purpose.
- Phase 2 · **El vacío llama** (at half PV). El Vértigo turns from the party to Uri: it reaches
  for Nil over two rounds. The twist: **Uri, who is very loyal, steps in front** of it. His eyes
  lose their violet for a moment and he speaks to it, his own words for once. From then on Altura
  **goes down one each round**, and with it El Vértigo's strength and guard.
- Phase 3 · **Bajar**. Every round Uri takes a step down the roof, Nil held tight; the fight ends
  when El Vértigo falls. Uri sits on the ridge, looks down and laughs: *"Pues no está tan alto."*
  His fear is beaten whatever the player did: the twist always happens.
- How the friend helps: phase 2, scripted. What the ánimas do: Papagato's cats shut the eye and
  keep their balance (proposal: Haru's claw on the spinning eye shuts it; whoever wears
  Papagato resists Mareo); Sangrado keeps working while El Vértigo is out of reach; No-ta and
  Matemágico as usual (whether El Vértigo carries pringue: _open_).
- Code: `Encounter` `uri-vertigo`, `FoeKind` `vertigo`; Altura as a fight resource, Mareo as a
  status (the combat core supports both, `docs/combat.md`).
- Numbers: to set with `just combat-sim` against Gerard, Aitor and the cats with no other ánimas.
