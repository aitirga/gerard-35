# El Nucli · Sants

Status: built (2026-10-31). Street zone `assets/geo/sants/` (`sants`), the shop's inside
`EL_NUCLI` in `src/environments.ts`, furnished by `src/nucli-scenery.ts` (`el-nucli`).

Where Gerard meets Bernat on the quiet (`docs/quests/bernat.md`: a spy feel, the city's people
acting strangely under the Queen, Bernat apparently immune). Two environments joined by a door:

- **`sants`**: the walk from the Plaça de Sants metro (L1, Riego entrance) up through the Plaça
  d'Osca, Carrer de Riego, the Passeig de Sant Antoni and Carrer de Santa Caterina to Carrer de
  Valladolid, where El Nucli is. Real map data (OpenStreetMap), turned so the shop faces the camera.
- **`el-nucli`**: the shop inside: walls of game boxes, demo tables with games half played, the
  counter, the tournament board, and a back room nobody sees from the street. Bernat's seat is there.

This doc is the place only: Bernat's mission, his lines and the phone call stay in his quest doc.

## The real place

**El Nucli is a real shop**: Carrer de Valladolid 41 (a ground-floor local), Sants, 08014
Barcelona. It sells Magic: the Gathering, other card games (Flesh and Blood, Lorcana), board games
for every kind of player and role-playing books, and it runs tournaments, drafts and release
presentations (its own site, October 2026). Its site carries the WPN Premium badge, the top tier
of Magic stores, which means a real play area. It moved to Valladolid from Carrer del Vallespir
24, where Time Out reviewed it in 2014 as *"la madriguera del ocio alternativo de Sants"*, with
board-game demos anyone could ask for. Its badge is a round wine-red disc with fanned cards drawn
in neon crimson lines and the motto *cau de l'oci* (the den of play).

What could not be checked: **no open photo of the shop's frontage or inside exists** (Commons has
none; the shop's site only shows product banners). The frontage and the inside below are designed
from the street, the building and how such shops are laid out. Aitor can compare them with Street
View (links below) or a visit.

The surroundings, from OpenStreetMap and openly licensed photos:

- **Carrer de Valladolid**: narrow and one-way, about 12 m from facade to facade, cars parked on
  the odd side, plane trees and a purple-leaved plum by number 43. Odd numbers on the north side,
  even on the south. Houses of 4 to 9 storeys (the cadastre parts in OSM carry `building:levels`):
  early-1900s houses in cream render scored as ashlar, stone surrounds, segmental arches on the
  ground floor, iron balconies full of plants, wooden shutters. El Nucli's building is 7 storeys;
  number 43 next door (Ten To Go Hostel) is 4. Also on the street: Pinyi Bicis, two sushi bars at
  40, a pharmacy at 10.
- **Across from El Nucli**: low parts of 2 to 3 storeys, then the **Palau Balañà** (Passeig de
  Sant Antoni 41-43), a cinema opened in 1965 and **closed since February 2020**: a long low box
  covered in white geometric relief tiles over a glazed entrance band, its letters still up
  (*PALAU BALAÑA MULTICINES 7 SALES*). The corner bar **Casa Astor** (Santa Caterina 6) faces the
  shop diagonally.
- **Passeig de Sant Antoni**: a diagonal boulevard with planted strips, benches and a two-way bike
  lane. Its north side is a wall of 8 to 12 storey blocks from the 1960s-70s with bars below (Via
  Fora!, Pub Gris, Disco Bar Joe's). The slim Cosmo Apartments tower (number 30) stands at its east
  end. It sits higher than Carrer de Riego: a ramp with railings joins them.
- **Carrer de Riego and the Plaça d'Osca**: Riego is a narrow single-surface pedestrian street of
  3-4 storey houses (some modernista, with curved iron balconies) and shop shutters painted as
  murals; the Plaça d'Osca beside it is a small square of bar terraces (Fo Bar, El Passadís,
  Seixanta Quaranta, Homo Sibaris, La Mestressa, a cooperative bar).
- **Carrer de Sants**: the neighbourhood's long shopping street, four lanes. The Plaça de Sants
  station has L1 vestibules under it (entrances Riego and Cros, Alcolea and Olzinelles) and L5 ones
  at the square: stairs between granite kerbs, black iron railings in a zigzag pattern, the sign
  with the line squares, white hexagonal tiles inside.
- **Recent changes**: Sants station (about 250 m east of the shop, off the map) is being remodelled
  in 2025-2028; the Plaça de Joan Peiró at the east end of Valladolid is fenced and being planted
  (the future Bosc de Viriat). The cinema has been empty since 2020.

## Identity

Status: refined from `docs/visual-style.md` (Sants: ochre, painted shutters, cool light; El Nucli:
bottle green, boxes and felt, warm light) after the references. Checked against the Meridiana
(pale 1970s blocks, wide grey avenue, works barriers, Hipercor blue), the Rec Comtal (brick, the
school's orange, the pink castle), Cardedeu (white render, terracotta roofs), Alegia (timber and
green hills), Plaça d'Espanya (red brick towers, monumental and open), the Barceloneta (sea light),
the Poble Sec (slopes and steps) and the Búnkers (dry earth and concrete in golden light). Sants is
the **narrow, lived-in ochre neighbourhood in shade, with one lit shop in it**. The colours with a
meaning stay out: no saturated pink (hers), no crimson (Noísta's), no violet (possession).

## How it was built

### The street: `assets/geo/sants/zone.json`

- **Frame**: anchor on El Nucli (41.3782469, 2.1372794); turned 30° so Carrer de Valladolid runs left
  to right and the shop's front (odd side) faces the camera; `bounds_turned` x −130 … 55 m, z −52 …
  345 m, its edges on streets (Alcolea, Badalona, Vallespir, Sants); 1.5 m cells: **123 × 265
  cells**, 288 buildings with heights from OSM `building:levels`, zoom 10.5. The map edge is the
  border, as everywhere. `lane_m` 3.0: streets tagged with their lanes keep them, so Carrer de Sants
  is four lanes (12 m) with their markings and Valladolid is one way for the traffic.
- **The route**: up the L1 stairs on Carrer de Sants (bottom left, spawn), up Carrer de Riego past
  the Plaça d'Osca, across the Passeig de Sant Antoni, straight up Carrer de Santa Caterina, which
  ends on Valladolid two doors from El Nucli. About 330 m: some 40 s walking.
- **Styles**: `default` (the ochre range, `sants` facades: scored render, stone surrounds, string
  courses, *persianes* or louvred shutters, iron balconies with pots; `sants-shops` ground floors:
  segmental arches over lit shops, street doors with fanlight grilles or shutters painted with
  murals; flat roofs whose colour is picked per building from a list), `block` (the Passeig's
  tall blocks, `flats`), `cosmo` (the dark tower), `cinema` (the Palau Balañà with its signature
  photo), `nucli` (the shop's building).
- **Landmarks** (`src/landmarks/`): `nucli-front` (the wine front, fascia with letters about 60 cm
  high, windows of boxes lit from inside, the door, the round sign on its iron bracket, the warm
  spill on the pavement), `terrassa` × 8 (the Plaça d'Osca's terraces: aluminium tables, chairs,
  full beers, white parasols, and `people` sitting at them), `assegut` × 8 (people on the benches),
  `fanal-osca` (the square's lamp), `contenidors` (the five containers on Valladolid).
- **Props**: the L1 totem and stair, plane trees (OSM's plus 6 more in the Plaça d'Osca and 13 on
  Valladolid and the Passeig), cars on Valladolid, lamps along the Passeig, 4 extra benches on the
  Passeig's promenade, and one on Carrer de Sants standing in for the map's bench that the four
  lanes cover (its two still people moved with it).
- **Still people**: `assegut` places block figures (the game's style: box body, round head, hair
  cap) on a bench, and `terrassa` seats them at its tables with `people`. Every head turns to
  `look` (120°: north-east in the turned frame, towards Trinitat Vella). Their parts cast no sun
  shadow and use few colours, to keep the draw calls down.

## Approximate or open

- **The shop's real frontage and inside were not seen** (no open photo exists): both are designed.
  Its name and address are real; its badge is not reproduced. The inside is wider than the real
  local and its furniture invented.
- **Draw calls**: about 1150–1300 following Gerard on the street and 1400 in the Plaça d'Osca
  (its trees and people), against the guide's ~1000; 820–870 in the shop. The overview (R) of the
  street costs about 7700 (the Meridiana's is about 10 000). Trees are the heaviest props.
- **The grid is flat**: the Passeig de Sant Antoni really stands above Carrer de Riego (a ramp with
  railings joins them).
- **The totem says L1**: the Riego vestibule is L1 only; the L5 platforms of Plaça de Sants are
  under the square, off the map.
- **Terrats are bare**: tiled in several colours but without water tanks, aerials or washing.
- **Real bars and shops** keep their real names on their signs, as on the Meridiana; the strange
  behaviour in the lines is the Queen's, never the businesses'.
- **Music** for the street and the shop: Aitor's call (no `PLACE_SONGS` entry yet).
