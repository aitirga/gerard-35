# El Nucli · Sants

Status: researched and drafted (2026-10-30). The street zone is `assets/geo/sants/`; the shop's
inside is planned below and not built yet.

Where Gerard meets Bernat on the quiet (`docs/quests/bernat.md`: a spy feel, the city's people
acting strangely under the Queen, Bernat apparently immune). Two environments joined by a door:

- **`sants`**: the walk from the Plaça de Sants metro (L1, Riego entrance) up through the Plaça
  d'Osca, Carrer de Riego, the Passeig de Sant Antoni and Carrer de Santa Caterina to Carrer de
  Valladolid, where El Nucli is. Real map data (OpenStreetMap), turned so the shop faces the camera.
- **`el-nucli`**: the shop inside: walls of game boxes, demo tables with games half played, the
  counter, the tournament board, and a back room nobody sees from the street. Bernat's seat is there.

This doc is the place only: Bernat's mission, his lines and the phone call stay in his quest doc.

## The real place

**El Nucli is a real shop**: Carrer de Valladolid 41 (a ground-floor local), Sants, 08014
Barcelona. It sells Magic: the Gathering, other card games (Flesh and Blood, Lorcana), board games
for every kind of player and role-playing books, and it runs tournaments, drafts and release
presentations (its own site, October 2026). Its site carries the WPN Premium badge, the top tier
of Magic stores, which means a real play area. It moved to Valladolid from Carrer del Vallespir
24, where Time Out reviewed it in 2014 as *"la madriguera del ocio alternativo de Sants"*, with
board-game demos anyone could ask for. Its badge is a round wine-red disc with fanned cards drawn
in neon crimson lines and the motto *cau de l'oci* (the den of play).

What could not be checked: **no open photo of the shop's frontage or inside exists** (Commons has
none; the shop's site only shows product banners). The frontage and the inside below are designed
from the street, the building and how such shops are laid out. Aitor can compare them with Street
View (links below) or a visit.

The surroundings, from OpenStreetMap and openly licensed photos:

- **Carrer de Valladolid**: narrow and one-way, about 12 m from facade to facade, cars parked on
  the odd side, plane trees and a purple-leaved plum by number 43. Odd numbers on the north side,
  even on the south. Houses of 4 to 9 storeys (the cadastre parts in OSM carry `building:levels`):
  early-1900s houses in cream render scored as ashlar, stone surrounds, segmental arches on the
  ground floor, iron balconies full of plants, wooden shutters. El Nucli's building is 7 storeys;
  number 43 next door (Ten To Go Hostel) is 4. Also on the street: Pinyi Bicis, two sushi bars at
  40, a pharmacy at 10.
- **Across from El Nucli**: low parts of 2 to 3 storeys, then the **Palau Balañà** (Passeig de
  Sant Antoni 41-43), a cinema opened in 1965 and **closed since February 2020**: a long low box
  covered in white geometric relief tiles over a glazed entrance band, its letters still up
  (*PALAU BALAÑA MULTICINES 7 SALES*). The corner bar **Casa Astor** (Santa Caterina 6) faces the
  shop diagonally.
- **Passeig de Sant Antoni**: a diagonal boulevard with planted strips, benches and a two-way bike
  lane. Its north side is a wall of 8 to 12 storey blocks from the 1960s-70s with bars below (Via
  Fora!, Pub Gris, Disco Bar Joe's). The slim Cosmo Apartments tower (number 30) stands at its east
  end. It sits higher than Carrer de Riego: a ramp with railings joins them.
- **Carrer de Riego and the Plaça d'Osca**: Riego is a narrow single-surface pedestrian street of
  3-4 storey houses (some modernista, with curved iron balconies) and shop shutters painted as
  murals; the Plaça d'Osca beside it is a small square of bar terraces (Fo Bar, El Passadís,
  Seixanta Quaranta, Homo Sibaris, La Mestressa, a cooperative bar).
- **Carrer de Sants**: the neighbourhood's long shopping street, four lanes. The Plaça de Sants
  station has L1 vestibules under it (entrances Riego and Cros, Alcolea and Olzinelles) and L5 ones
  at the square: stairs between granite kerbs, black iron railings in a zigzag pattern, the sign
  with the line squares, white hexagonal tiles inside.
- **Recent changes**: Sants station (about 250 m east of the shop, off the map) is being remodelled
  in 2025-2028; the Plaça de Joan Peiró at the east end of Valladolid is fenced and being planted
  (the future Bosc de Viriat). The cinema has been empty since 2020.

## Identity card

Checked against the places that exist or are being built: the Meridiana (pale 1970s blocks, wide
grey avenue, works barriers, Hipercor blue), the Rec Comtal (brick, the school's orange, the pink
castle), Cardedeu (white render, terracotta roofs), Alegia (timber and green hills), Plaça d'Espanya
(red brick towers, monumental and open), the Barceloneta (sea light, laundry), the Poble Sec
(slopes and steps under Montjuïc), the Búnkers (dry earth and concrete in golden light). Sants is
the **narrow, lived-in ochre neighbourhood in shade, with one lit shop in it**. The card follows the
style guide's first proposal (Sants: ochre `#cfa463`, painted shutters and old render, cool light;
El Nucli: bottle green `#2f4a3a`, boxes and felt, warm light) and refines it from the references.
The colours with a meaning stay out: no saturated pink (hers), no crimson (Noísta's).

## Build plan

After the foundation (materials pipeline, landmark modules, `docs/visual-style.md`) is merged in:

1. Re-read `docs/visual-style.md`, `tools/textures.py` and `src/landmarks/`; fit the identity card

9. Report fragment to *hecho*.

## Approximate or open

- The shop's real frontage and inside were not seen; both are designed. Its name is real, its logo
  is not reproduced.
- The inside is wider than the real local, and the furniture is invented.
- The grid is flat: the Passeig de Sant Antoni really stands above Carrer de Riego.
- The metro totem says L1 (the Riego vestibule is L1 only); the station also has the L5 at the
  square, off the map.
- Real bars and shops keep their real names on their signs, as on the Meridiana; the strange
  behaviour in the lines is the Queen's, never the businesses'.
- Zone size: 123 × 265 cells and 288 buildings, the biggest Barcelona zone so far (the Meridiana
  has 52, Cardedeu 169); the guide's budget is about 1000 draw calls, so the courtyard parts may
  need merging or dropping, and the buildings batching, before the build is done.
- Music for the street and the shop: Aitor's call.
