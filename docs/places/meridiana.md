# Avinguda Meridiana · Fabra i Puig to Hipercor (`meridiana-calle`)

The street outside Gerard and Aitor's flat, and the first place the player walks in chapter 2.
Today's scene ([barcelona.md](../barcelona.md#designed-zones)) has the right blocks, shops and
works, but a drawn look and an invented cross-section. This file is the research and the plan to
redo it with real materials and real proportions, and to compare it side by side with today's
(`docs/story-outline.md`, "First step, before any new zone").

Status (2026-10-30): **research and draft**. `zone.json` has the new reference notes and
viewpoints; nothing the build reads has changed yet. The cross-section below was drafted and
built in a scratch copy only (its preview is in the places report).

## The place

- **What it is.** Barcelona's long avenue to the north-east, here in Sant Andreu, between the
  Fabra i Puig crossing (L1, the Rodalies station under the avenue, the intercity coaches) and
  Hipercor (Meridiana 350–356). About 345 m of it is played, from the even side, where the shops
  are, to the odd side across six lanes.
- **The works.** This stretch is the Felip II–Fabra i Puig phase of the Meridiana reform, begun in
  April 2025 for 18 months (€26 M): two general lanes and a bus lane each way, a two-way bike lane
  in a central planted median with a double row of trees, sidewalks widened with planted beds,
  about 275 new trees and 7,200 m² of beds, crossings from 9 to 15 along the phase. In October
  2026, when Aitor photographed it at night, it was in its last weeks: new black asphalt, young
  staked trees, red and white barriers and rubble sacks still about.
- **The blocks.** 1960s–70s flats of 8 to 11 storeys (OpenStreetMap `building:levels`), cream
  render or tan facing brick, continuous balcony slabs with white or aluminium railings, enclosed
  aluminium galleries, teal-green and grey canvas awnings, set-back top floors, bare brick party
  walls (*mitgeres*) where heights change, and flat roofs (*terrats*) crowded with stair and lift
  housings, water tanks, aerials and air conditioners. Shops on every ground floor.
- **Hipercor.** A long low box (a Sears store in the 1970s): light panels with thin blue vertical
  stripes under a white scalloped canopy, a row of shallow vaults, with HIPERCOR in blue on a
  white sign; recycling containers and benches in front. Behind and above it rise rental slabs of
  15–17 storeys (OSM `relation/8938011`, `8938019`, `8938017`) with dark brown spandrels and white
  ribbon windows. The "dark tower" of the night photos is the nearest of them.
- **Under the avenue.** The L1 and the Rodalies tunnel (R3, R4, R7). The Renfe station is now
  *Barcelona-Fabra i Puig* (it was Sant Andreu Arenal); the L1 also comes up at Concepció Arenal.
- **What a local recognises.** The dead-straight avenue and its endless parallel bands; plane
  trees with pale mottled trunks; curved grey poles with yellow traffic lights; the five-colour
  row of recycling containers by the kerb; motorbikes parked on the sidewalk; Bicing docks; the
  Granier and the other shop signs; Hipercor's canopy and blue letters; TMB red buses and the white
  intercity coaches.
- **Left out on purpose.** The 1987 bombing memorial and the avenue's protests: nothing tragic or
  political in a birthday game.

## Layout

The zone stays as it is: 345 × 130 m around the anchor (41.429, 2.1841), turned −62° with
`bounds_turned` so the avenue runs left to right, Fabra i Puig on the left and Hipercor on the
right, the even side's fronts facing the camera; 1.5 m cells (230 × 87); spawn by home's portal;
the L1 entrance on home's corner; zebras at x −67 (Carrer de la Jota), 30 (Escòcia and Dublín)
and 104 (Hipercor). Doors and places stay (home's portal, the station, the shops, Hipercor, the
works, the hoarding).

## Build plan

After the shared foundation (materials pipeline, `src/landmarks/`, `docs/visual-style.md`) merges:

1. **Before.** Keep today's captures (`output/screenshots/meridiana-before`, `traffic-before`) for
   the side-by-side.
2. **Cross-section.** Redraw `sketch.json` to the six lanes above, move the props, places, labels
   and scenario cells, `just geo-build meridiana-calle`, check the preview and that every place is
   reachable; `npm test` for the environment and traffic tests (traffic queues, the bus lane, the
   lights at the three crossings, cars stopping for Gerard).
3. **Ground.** `panot-gris` on the sidewalks at 20 cm tiles, `granit` kerbs with a real 12 cm step
   (the carriageways a step down, people and cars following a road mask, since scenes have no
   ground heights; if that proves costly, a granite strip with a shadow line instead), `asfalt-nou`
   on every lane, `terra` with `fulles` in pits and beds, cast-iron grates round the pits.
4. **Blocks.** Ground floor 4.2 m (shops and a mezzanine band), upper storeys 2.9 m; the facade
   bay at about 3.4 m (today 2.7 m); balcony slabs and galleries as real geometry (one batched mesh
   per building) so the facades carry shadow lines; a 0.4 m cornice and a 1 m parapet; set-back
   top floors on blocks of nine storeys or more; bare brick or grey render on walls that rise above
   a lower neighbour (*mitgeres*); `mao-groc` and `estuc` instead of flat colours.
5. **Roofs.** `rajola` terraces with stair and lift housings, water tanks, aerials, satellite
   dishes, air conditioners and the odd washing line, instanced and seeded by building, so the
   overview reads as Barcelona from above.
6. **Hipercor and the slabs.** The scalloped canopy as geometry (a row of shallow vaults about 6 m
   each), blue stripes, the sign; the brown-banded slab as the landmark behind it.
7. **Furniture.** Two new prop kinds: a row of five recycling containers by the kerb and a Bicing
   dock; benches and chairs on the wider sidewalk, a café terrace outside Café Entrepans, parked
   motorbikes; the vent grates on the median.
8. **The edge.** Replace the green hill blobs behind the blocks with low hazy city massing.
9. **Effects.** Rain darkens and glosses the asphalt and panot towards their wet twins and leaves
   puddles by the kerbs that dry afterwards (`docs/graphics.md` § What next, 1); at dusk the open
   shops spill warm light on the panot (light pools, not real lights) under the cold LED lamps;
   plane leaves fall and settle; cyclists and scooters on the bike lane, motorbikes in the traffic;
   a train's rumble and a puff from the median's grates now and then.
10. **Check and compare.** `just typecheck`, `npm test`, then `just screenshots meridiana`,
    `traffic`, `weather` (add a Meridiana rain shot) and `street-music` (`--chrome`), each with
    `--gpu`; the same shots before and after in the places report; draw calls with
    `just screenshots gpu-perf --gpu --perf` against today's ~1000.

Nothing here adds a story flag, a place or a fight, so the development picker keeps its
`meridiana-calle` entry as it is.
